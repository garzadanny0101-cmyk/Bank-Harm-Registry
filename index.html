Trust architecture

Facts before conclusions.

  • Verified means a primary source directly supports the statement.
  • Reported means credible reporting exists but primary confirmation may be incomplete.
  • Alleged means a complaint, pleading or accusation—not an adjudicated fact.
  • Complaint counts and search results are context, not proof of wrongdoing.
