Important

Support does not buy legal services.

Bank Harm Registry does not represent contributions as tax deductible unless qualified nonprofit status is confirmed. Support does not create representation or guarantee help or a result in any case.

Transparency

Donors do not control report decisions.

Donors cannot access private reports, contact information, moderation decisions, publication decisions, support matching, testimony, or case strategy.

Credential support

Future support may fund education, credential verification, research tools, conflict screening, and qualified expert review. It must never pay for predetermined testimony or a particular outcome.
